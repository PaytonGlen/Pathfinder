LCD
===

The display is a 16x2 LCD with an I2C backpack.

Where it is:
- connected to the slave Arduino
- uses I2C on A4 and A5

What it shows:
- light sensor value
- temperature
- MPU data sent from the master

Common LCD address:
- usually `0x27`
- sometimes `0x3F`

If the screen looks wrong:
- adjust the contrast pot on the back
- if nothing shows up, double-check the I2C address
