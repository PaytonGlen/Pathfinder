================================================================
  07 - DISPLAY (16x2 LCD)
================================================================

The little blue screen on top of the tank that shows live
readings. Lives on the SLAVE Arduino.


16x2 LCD WITH PCF8574 I2C BACKPACK
----------------------------------
"16x2" = 16 characters wide, 2 rows tall.

Two parts physically soldered together:

  1) THE LCD ITSELF
     - HD44780-compatible character LCD
     - Has a built-in adjustable contrast pot on the back
     - Backlight is controllable from code

  2) THE I2C BACKPACK
     - A small board with a PCF8574 chip soldered behind the LCD
     - PCF8574 is an 8-bit I/O expander
     - WHY IT MATTERS: a raw HD44780 LCD needs 6+ wires to the
       Arduino. The backpack converts that into just 4 wires
       using the I2C protocol. Massive wiring win.

KEY SPECS:
  * Communication: I2C
  * I2C address: 0x27   (typical, can vary)
  * Power: 5 V (drawn from the slave Arduino)


PINS ON SLAVE
-------------
  A4 -> SDA  (I2C data)
  A5 -> SCL  (I2C clock)

  (Same I2C bus as the MPU on the master side. Each board has
   its own I2C bus, so no conflict between them.)


WHAT IT SHOWS
-------------
Right now the LCD displays a few rotating values:
  * Live photoresistor reading (light level)
  * Temperature in degrees F (from the TMP36)
  * Accelerometer X/Y/Z values streamed from the master

For Project 3 we may add more readouts — we'll re-plan layout
once we see the new rubric.


GOTCHAS
-------
  * Contrast: if the screen looks blank or full of black boxes,
    twist the little blue potentiometer on the back of the
    backpack. That's contrast, not a code bug.
  * I2C address can be 0x27 OR 0x3F depending on the unit. If
    "nothing shows up" check the address with an I2C scanner
    sketch.
