Light And Temp Sensors
======================

Both of these sensors are on the slave Arduino.

Photoresistor:
- used for light level
- connected to A0
- read as a normal analog value

TMP36:
- used for temperature
- connected to A1
- also read as an analog value

What we do with them:
- show both on the LCD
- they are not part of the actual driving logic right now
