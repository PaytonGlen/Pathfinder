================================================================
  06 - ENVIRONMENTAL SENSORS (LIGHT + TEMPERATURE)
================================================================

Two simple analog sensors that monitor the world around the
tank. Both live on the SLAVE Arduino and feed values to the
LCD display.


PHOTORESISTOR / LDR (LIGHT SENSOR)
----------------------------------
A "light dependent resistor". Resistance CHANGES based on how
much light is hitting it.

  * Dark   -> high resistance
  * Bright -> low resistance

We wire it as a VOLTAGE DIVIDER with a fixed resistor, so the
analog pin sees a voltage that changes with light level.

KEY SPECS:
  * Output range (after voltage divider, on A0):
      ~50    in near-total darkness
      ~1000  in bright direct light

PIN ON SLAVE:  A0  (analog read, 0-1023)

WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
Live reading on the LCD. Could also drive behaviors like
"slow down in the dark" if we wanted.


TMP36 TEMPERATURE SENSOR
------------------------
Analog Devices temperature sensor in a tiny 3-pin TO-92 package
(looks like a transistor).

KEY SPECS:
  * Output: 10 mV per degree C, with a 500 mV OFFSET
            (so 0 C = 0.5 V, 25 C = 0.75 V, 100 C = 1.5 V)
  * Range:  -40 C to +125 C
  * Accuracy: +/- 2 C (uncalibrated)

PIN ON SLAVE:  A1  (analog read)


HOW WE CONVERT THE READING
--------------------------
We display temperature in Fahrenheit on the LCD. To avoid the
slow floating-point math on an Arduino, we use integer math
and store the value as "tenths of a degree F":

    T_x10_F = (((analog * 5000 / 1024) - 500) * 9 / 5) + 320

Then we divide by 10 when displaying.


WHY THESE TWO ON THE SLAVE?
---------------------------
They're slow, low-priority sensors and they don't affect
driving decisions. Perfect work for the "recorder" Arduino
that has time to format strings and update the LCD.
