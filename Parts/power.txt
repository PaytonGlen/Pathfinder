================================================================
  09 - POWER SYSTEM
================================================================

The two Arduinos have SEPARATE power supplies. This is on
purpose — both for the rubric requirement and to keep noise
from the motors out of the slave's sensitive sensors.


MASTER + MOTORS (one shared supply)
-----------------------------------
  * Source: rechargeable battery pack mounted on the kit
  * Connects through the motor driver shield
  * Powers:
      - The master Arduino (via Vin through the shield)
      - The TB6612FNG motor driver
      - The drive motors themselves
  * One on/off switch controls all of the above


SLAVE ARDUINO (separate supply)
-------------------------------
  * Source: 9V battery
  * Connects via the slave Arduino's barrel jack
  * Powers:
      - The slave Arduino
      - The LCD (drawn from the slave's 5V rail)
      - The slave's photoresistor, TMP36, side ultrasonic
  * Runtime: roughly 5-8 hours of continuous use
  * NOTE: Required by the project rubric to be on its own
    battery (not the kit's main pack).


COMMON GROUND
-------------
Even though the two Arduinos have separate power supplies, we
tie their GROUND pins together with a wire (this is part of
the UART cable). Without a common ground reference, the serial
signals between them won't be readable.

Power supplies separate. Grounds connected. That's the rule.


OPTIONAL: NEOPIXEL STRIP
------------------------
If we add a NeoPixel LED strip, it should be powered from a
THIRD source (we've been using an 18650 Li-ion power bank).

Why isolate it: NeoPixels pull current in sharp spikes when
they change color, which can momentarily brown out the slave
Arduino and reset it.

Still tie the NeoPixel's GND to the slave's GND so the data
signal works.


QUICK REFERENCE TABLE
---------------------
  SUBSYSTEM            POWER SOURCE
  -------------------  ----------------------------------------
  Master + motors      Kit rechargeable battery pack (via shield)
  Slave Arduino        9V battery via barrel jack
  LCD + slave sensors  Slave Arduino's 5V rail
  (Optional NeoPixels) Separate 18650 power bank
