Power
=====

The two Arduinos use separate power sources.

Master side:
- powered from the main battery pack on the tank
- that powers the master Arduino, motor driver, and drive motors

Slave side:
- powered from a separate 9V battery
- that powers the slave Arduino, LCD, and slave sensors

Important rule:
- the boards still need a common ground
- even with separate power, their GND pins need to be connected for UART to work
