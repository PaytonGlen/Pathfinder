IMU
===

We use an MPU-6050 on a GY-521 breakout board.

What it gives us:
- 3-axis accelerometer
- 3-axis gyroscope

Where it is:
- connected to the master Arduino
- uses I2C
- A4 is SDA
- A5 is SCL

How we use it:
- the master reads the raw MPU values
- it sends them to the slave in a line like `M,ax,ay,az,gx,gy,gz`
- the slave can show part of that data on the LCD

Default I2C address:
- `0x68`
