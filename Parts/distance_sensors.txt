Distance Sensors
================

We use three HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors and one Sharp IR sensor.

Ultrasonic sensors:
- front ultrasonic on the master
  - used to stop when something is directly ahead
  - pins: D13 trig, D12 echo
- scan arm ultrasonic on the master
  - used to look left and right after stopping
  - pins: A1 trig, A2 echo
- side ultrasonic on the slave
  - used to watch the side of the tank
  - if something gets too close, the slave sends `OBSTACLE`
  - pins: D8 trig, D7 echo

IR sensor:
- Sharp GP2Y0A41SK0F
- mounted on the scan arm next to the arm ultrasonic
- used for short-range obstacle checks
- master pin: A0

Why both ultrasonic and IR:
- ultrasonic is better for longer range
- IR is better for short-range stuff and thinner objects
- using both helps with obstacle detection
