UART Link
=========

The two Arduinos talk over hardware serial.

Settings:
- baud rate: 9600
- line-based text messages

Wiring:
- master D1 goes to slave D0
- slave D1 goes to master D0
- master GND goes to slave GND

Messages:
- master to slave:
  - `M,ax,ay,az,gx,gy,gz`
- slave to master:
  - `OBSTACLE`

Important upload note:
- D0 and D1 are also the USB serial pins
- unplug the serial cross-wires before uploading code
- reconnect them after uploading
