================================================================
  02 - DRIVE MOTORS (MAKES THE TANK MOVE)
================================================================

Two parts work together to drive the tank:
  1) The motor driver shield (the "muscle controller")
  2) The DC gear motors (the actual "muscles")

Both belong to the MASTER Arduino's subsystem.


TB6612FNG MOTOR DRIVER SHIELD (ELEGOO V1.1)
-------------------------------------------
This sits on top of the master Arduino like a hat. It contains
a Toshiba TB6612FNG dual H-bridge chip.

WHY WE NEED IT:
The Arduino's pins can only output ~20 mA each. The motors need
HUNDREDS of mA. The motor driver acts like a power amplifier
and direction controller. The Arduino tells it "go forward at
70% speed" and the driver handles the actual current.

KEY SPECS:
  * Drives 2 motors independently (PWM speed + direction)
  * Motor voltage: 4.5 - 13.5 V
  * Current: 1.2 A continuous per channel (3.2 A peak)
  * Has built-in flyback diodes + thermal shutdown
  * STBY pin lets us shut the motors off for low power

PINS USED ON MASTER:
  D3  -> STBY  (motor enable/disable)
  D5  -> PWMA  (right motor speed, 0-255)
  D6  -> PWMB  (left motor speed, 0-255)
  D7  -> AIN1  (right motor direction)
  D8  -> BIN1  (left motor direction)


2x DC GEAR MOTORS
-----------------
These are the actual motors spinning the rubber tracks.

KEY SPECS:
  * 6 V nominal
  * Internally geared down -> low speed, high torque (good
    for moving a tank)
  * Speed control by PWM duty cycle (0 = stopped, 255 = full)

CALIBRATION NOTE (IMPORTANT):
The two motors don't spin at exactly the same speed even when
given the same PWM. We measured and tuned:

    LEFT_DRIVE_SPEED  = 173
    RIGHT_DRIVE_SPEED = 180

These compensate for the natural mismatch and make the tank
drive straight. If you ever change motors, you'll need to
re-tune these.


HOW TURNING WORKS
-----------------
There's no steering wheel. Like a real tank, we use
DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE:

  * Left track faster than right  -> turns right
  * Right track faster than left  -> turns left
  * Tracks spin opposite directions -> tank spins in place

Since we don't have wheel encoders, turn angles are TIME-BASED.
We use calibrated constants MS_PER_FOOT and MS_PER_90_DEG that
we measured on hard floor.
